Pink Cloud · design system · components

Buttons + motion

One button family and one flat toggle, sharing one motion system. Hover the buttons: the label slides out and back in (the motion from your reference video). Press: the button gets smaller and darker, then springs back. No shadows, no 3D.

01 · Try it

Live buttons. Hover, press and hold, and tab to them with the keyboard.

04 · Motion

One motion system for the whole UI. Only transform, opacity and color move, so everything stays on the compositor at 60fps. Nothing animates layout.

InteractionWhat movesDuration · easing
Hover in / outLabel slides out one side while its twin slides in from the other, clipped by the button. Fill steps one shade darker--pc-motion-hover 240ms · --pc-ease-spring. Color 150ms · --pc-ease-out
Pressscale(.96) + fill one more shade darker--pc-motion-press 90ms · --pc-ease-out
ReleaseSprings back to scale(1) with a small overshoot--pc-motion-release 220ms · --pc-ease-spring
ToggleThumb slides; option text fades to primary; pressed option scales to .97--pc-motion-toggle 260ms · --pc-ease-spring
Touch devicesNo hover swap (@media (hover:hover)). Press still scales-
Reduced motionNo transforms, no swap. Colors still changeprefers-reduced-motion: reduce
--pc-ease-outcubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1) · press, color, anything responding
--pc-ease-springcubic-bezier(.34,1.36,.64,1) · hover swap, release, thumb (~8% overshoot)
--pc-ease-in-outcubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1) · things already on screen moving somewhere new
Hover, frozen → → 0ms · ~110ms · 240ms

Values measured from the reference video (30fps): the swap travels in about 100-130ms, overshoots a little, and settles by about 230ms. The video shows no press scale, so the press values (.96, 90ms in, 220ms spring out) follow your "smaller and darker" note.
